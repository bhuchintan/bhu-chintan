About Us

BHU Chintan is a student-driven initiative at Banaras Hindu University that promotes intellectual discussions, research, and reforms. It aims to be a platform for students to engage in constructive dialogue and thought leadership rooted in Indian values and global ideas.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To nurture a community of thinkers and changemakers.

Mission:

  • Provide a platform for students to express ideas and publish research.
  • Encourage policy debates and social initiatives.
  • Bridge academics and real-world action.

What We Do

🗣️ Discussion Circles

Weekly forums to debate and discuss contemporary issues.

📝 Articles & Journals

Original student writings and academic essays published regularly.

🎙️ Podcasts & Talks

Conversations with thought leaders and university alumni.

🧪 Research Projects

Collaborative, interdisciplinary research by student groups.
